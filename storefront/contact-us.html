Before you write

You will get a faster answer if you include:

  • Your order number, if your question is about an order
  • The email address you ordered with
  • Photographs, if something arrived damaged or does not look right

What we can help with

Order status — where your parcel is and when it should arrive. Tracking updates can lag behind the parcel itself, so if the tracking looks stuck for more than a couple of days, ask us.

Changing an order — we can usually amend an address or cancel an item if it has not shipped. Write immediately and we will try.

Returns — email us with your order number and we will confirm the return address. Please do not send anything back before we reply, or we may not be able to match it to your order.

Something arrived damaged — send photographs of the item and the packaging. We resolve these ourselves rather than sending you to the carrier.

Product questions — dimensions, materials, compatibility, or whether something suits what you actually need. If it does not, we will tell you.

Advice before buying — describe what you are trying to do and we will point you at the right option, including a cheaper one if that is the right answer.
